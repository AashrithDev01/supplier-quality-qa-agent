# PROC-02 — Lot blocking and unblocking

## 1. Blocking criteria

A lot is blocked as soon as at least one of the following conditions is met:

- at least one defect of severity 4 or 5 is recorded on the lot;
- the lot defect rate exceeds 2% (sum of defective quantities divided by the
  quantity received).

## 2. Unblocking

Unblocking is approved by the Plant Quality Manager, based on the supplier's
action plan. For a severity 5 defect, approval by the Quality Director is
also required.

## 3. Calculation conventions

- Cost of non-quality is expressed in euros. Internal conversion:
  1 USD = 0.92 EUR.
- Dimensional deviations are analysed in millimetres.
