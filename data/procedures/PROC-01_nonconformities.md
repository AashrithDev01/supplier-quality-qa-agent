# PROC-01 — Handling of supplier non-conformities

## 1. Severity scale

| Severity | Definition |
|---|---|
| 1 | Minor cosmetic defect, no functional impact |
| 2 | Tolerated deviation, slightly out of tolerance |
| 3 | Minor functional deviation |
| 4 | Major functional deviation |
| 5 | Safety risk or regulatory non-compliance |

## 2. Supplier response deadlines

- Severity 5: initial response (8D) within 24 hours.
- Severity 4: response within 3 working days.
- Severity 1 to 3: response within 10 working days.

## 3. Escalation

Beyond the deadline, the non-conformity is escalated to the supplier's
Purchasing Manager. After two unanswered reminders, it is escalated to the
Quality Director.
